# Legal and regulatory notice

ORBITT PAY is software for self-hosted payment processing. This repository and its documentation do not provide legal, tax, compliance, or financial advice. Using non-custodial software does not by itself determine whether an operator, merchant, or service provider needs an authorization, registration, notification, or other compliance controls.

Regulatory treatment depends on the real service and its contracts, funds and crypto-asset flows, who controls each step, the assets involved, where the parties operate, and how the product is marketed. In the EU, MiCA defines crypto-asset services and establishes authorization requirements and exceptions for providers. Germany also has national payment-services and financial-services rules. For example, BaFin says the need for permission for payment processing depends on how it is structured and contracted, and that a provider supplying only technical services may qualify for an exception in some arrangements. These are context-dependent assessments, not blanket conclusions about this software.

Before operating ORBITT PAY commercially, obtain advice from qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction. Have counsel review the operator's role, custody and control, payment flow, merchant terms, consumer disclosures, sanctions and AML obligations, privacy, tax, and any applicable licensing or notification requirements.

**Legal review required before commercial deployment.** This notice is general information only and is not legal advice.
